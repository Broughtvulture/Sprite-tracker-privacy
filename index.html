Sprite Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Sprite Tracker is an unofficial fan-made companion app for tracking Sprite collection progress and finding other users with complementary collections.

Information stored on your device

Your Sprite collection and mastery progress are stored locally on your device. The app also stores the Fortnite player name you enter so you do not have to re-enter it each time.

Online matchmaking data

When you use Find Sprite Players, the app uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. It sends an anonymous Firebase user identifier, Sprite availability, queue status, and timestamps so compatible users can be matched.

Fortnite player name privacy

Your Fortnite player name is not included in the public matchmaking queue. For a specific match request, your name is stored in a protected reveal record. The app only permits the other participant to read that record after both participants accept the same request.

Sharing

Collection images are created locally on your device and do not contain your Fortnite player name. When you choose Share, Android opens its system sharing interface and you select the destination app. The destination service processes the shared image according to its own privacy practices.

Service providers

Sprite Tracker uses Google Firebase services for anonymous authentication and online matchmaking. Firebase may process technical and service data as described in Google's applicable privacy documentation.

Data retention

Queue state is updated as you enter and leave matchmaking. Match request records may remain in Cloud Firestore for service operation, troubleshooting, and maintenance until they are deleted or replaced by app operation or backend maintenance.

Children

Sprite Tracker is not designed to collect more personal information than is needed for its collection-tracking and matchmaking features. Users should follow the age requirements and parental controls applicable to Fortnite, Google Play, and their device.

Fan content notice

Sprite Tracker is an unofficial fan-made companion app. It is not an official Epic Games product and is not endorsed by Epic Games.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information shown on Sprite Tracker's Google Play listing.
