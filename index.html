Sprite Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Sprite Tracker is an unofficial fan-made companion app for tracking Sprite collection progress and finding other users with complementary collections.

Information stored on your device

Your Sprite collection and mastery progress are stored locally on your device. The app also stores the Fortnite player name you enter so you do not have to re-enter it each time.

App preferences such as your selected language and light or dark theme may also be stored locally on your device.

Online matchmaking data

When you use Find Sprite Players, the app uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. It sends an anonymous Firebase user identifier, Sprite availability, matchmaking queue status, and timestamps so compatible users can be matched.

Technical information and approximate location

Firebase Authentication and related Google Firebase services may process technical information such as your IP address, user-agent information, device or service metadata, and other information needed to operate and secure the service.

An IP address may also be used to infer an approximate geographic location, such as a general city or regional area. Sprite Tracker does not request or collect your device's precise GPS location for its collection-tracking or matchmaking features.

This technical information may be processed for authentication, security, fraud and abuse prevention, service operation, and compliance purposes.

Fortnite player name privacy

Your Fortnite player name is not included in the public matchmaking queue. For a specific match request, your name is stored in a protected reveal record. The app only permits the other participant to read that record after both participants accept the same request.

Sharing

Collection images are created locally on your device and do not contain your Fortnite player name. When you choose Share, Android opens its system sharing interface and you select the destination app.

The destination service processes the shared image according to its own privacy practices.

Service providers

Sprite Tracker uses Google Firebase services for anonymous authentication and online matchmaking. Firebase acts as a service provider supporting the app's online functionality and may process technical and service data according to Google's applicable privacy and Firebase documentation.

Data retention

Queue state is updated as you enter and leave matchmaking. Match request records and related service records may remain in Cloud Firestore for service operation, troubleshooting, security, and maintenance until they are deleted or replaced through app operation or backend maintenance.

Data deletion

Sprite Tracker does not currently provide an in-app or web-based account deletion system because users do not create a traditional Sprite Tracker account. Local collection data can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Children

Sprite Tracker is not designed to collect more personal information than is needed for its collection-tracking and matchmaking features. Users should follow the age requirements and parental controls applicable to Fortnite, Google Play, and their device.
